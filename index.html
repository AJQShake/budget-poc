Budget Drive POC demo mode

One-time setup

Paste the OAuth Client ID from Google Cloud (see README). It is not a secret; it is stored in this browser only. You can also put it in config.js.

Just want to see it work? Try demo mode with no Google account: open ?mock=alice in one tab and ?mock=bob in another.

Sign in

Sign in with the Google account whose Drive holds (or will hold) the shared folder.

Shared folder

Encryption (optional)

Applies on the next save. Google then only sees scrambled data. If you forget the passphrase, the data cannot be recovered.

Share with your partner

Gives them edit access to the folder. Google emails them a link. They then open this same app and sign in.
